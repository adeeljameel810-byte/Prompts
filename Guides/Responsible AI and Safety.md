# Responsible AI and Safety

Use prompts responsibly and review generated output before acting on it.

## Protect information

- Do not include passwords, API keys, private documents, or unnecessary personal data.
- Remove confidential details before sharing examples.
- Use synthetic or redacted data when testing.

## Review high-impact output

Treat responses about health, law, finance, employment, safety, or security as starting points for research—not as a substitute for qualified professional advice. Verify important claims against reliable sources.

## Security boundaries

Do not use prompts to bypass access controls, expose private information, create malware, or facilitate harm. For cybersecurity prompts, focus on defensive testing, authorized environments, risk reduction, and responsible disclosure.

## Human oversight

Check factual accuracy, bias, citations, assumptions, and unintended consequences. Keep a human decision-maker responsible for consequential actions.
