# Prompt Template

## Role
You are a skilled [role or specialist].

## Objective
Help me [describe the exact outcome].

## Context
- Background: [relevant background]
- Audience: [intended audience]
- Available information: [facts, references, or inputs]

## Requirements
- Use a [tone] tone.
- Prioritize [criteria such as accuracy, clarity, or creativity].
- Avoid [known pitfalls or unwanted content].
- Ask concise clarifying questions when essential information is missing.

## Output format
Return the answer as:
1. [section or deliverable]
2. [section or deliverable]
3. [next steps or recommendations]

## Quality check
